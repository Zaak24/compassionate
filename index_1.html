The challenge

A complete caregiver folder has ten items, and several renew every year.

Paperwork is scattered

IDs, TB results, CPR cards and CCHS forms live in folders, inboxes and phones.

Gaps are found late

A missing background check or an expired renewal is spotted only when someone checks the folder.

Matching takes time

Reading many applications to find the right caregiver for each client is slow work.

Meet Zaak

An AI assistant built into both journeys.

Job description writer

Enter the title, pay and location. Zaak drafts a clear posting to edit.

Applicant sorting in plain words

Type “4+ stars, 5 years, CPR, complete folder” and see only those applicants.

Candidate match

A score, strengths, items to verify and interview questions for each applicant.

Job finder for caregivers

Ranks the best open jobs for a caregiver and flags any missing requirement.

Built-in safeguards: Zaak ignores age, race, religion, gender, disability and criminal history, and hiring decisions stay with your team.

Technology on AWS

A serverless design that is simple to run and grows with you.

Users

Web app

Agencies and caregivers on any phone or computer

Edge

CloudFront and S3

Fast delivery with free HTTPS certificates

Logic

Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda

Sign-in, roles and the app’s rules, paid per request

Data

DynamoDB and private S3

Caregiver records in a database; documents in locked storage

Also: SES for email notifications, and Zaak’s AI running as a backend function with its keys kept on the server.

CareConnect × Compassionate Companions

How files are protected

IDs and certificates stay locked, encrypted and logged.

Private storage

Documents sit in a private S3 bucket with all public access blocked.

Encrypted

Encrypted at rest with a managed key, and in transit with HTTPS.

Expiring links

Files open through short-lived signed links, valid for about five minutes.

Role-based access

An agency sees only documents from caregivers who applied to its jobs.

Audit trail

Every document view is logged, so you can see who opened what and when.

Protected sign-in

Managed accounts with password reset and multi-factor sign-in, plus a web firewall.

Collect less: ID images are requested after an offer, deleted on a retention schedule, and Social Security numbers are never typed into the app.
